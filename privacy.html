Privacy Policy

Metanoia: Bible Affirmations · Last updated 20 November 2026

Metanoia plays King James Bible audio on your iPhone. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts.

What stays on your phone

  • Your onboarding answers, the name you type, your journal entries, check-ins, listening hours and reminder times are stored on your device.
  • If you choose the option about a specific sin or struggle, that answer never leaves your phone.

What we collect

  • Usage analytics (PostHog): which screens you see and tap, your answers to onboarding questions (except the one above), and a random app ID. We use this to improve the app.
  • Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat): your subscription status and purchase history, tied to a random app ID, so your access works and can be restored. We never see your card details; Apple handles payment.
  • Advertising measurement (Meta, TikTok, Google Firebase): whether the app was installed or a subscription started after an ad. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, these services may use your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, they receive no advertising identifier, and Apple's privacy-preserving attribution is used instead.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data.
  • We don't read your journal or listen to anything you say. The app never uses your microphone.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings › Notifications.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To ask us to delete analytics or purchase records linked to your app ID, email us.

Children

Metanoia is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.
